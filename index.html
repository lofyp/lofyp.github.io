1. Information We Handle

GPScope does not require you to create an account. The App requests only the device access needed for its features. Some information remains on your device, while limited analytics information is transmitted to Microsoft Clarity as described below.

Location data

With your permission, the App accesses precise location readings such as latitude, longitude, altitude, speed, course, heading, and GPS accuracy to show them on the dashboard. GPScope does not store a location history or send precise latitude and longitude to our own servers.

Motion and pressure data

On supported devices, the App accesses motion-sensor and barometer readings to display atmospheric pressure and relative altitude. These readings are processed for App functionality.

Photos you select

If you choose a custom background, the App receives only the photo you select through Apple's system photo picker. A resized copy is stored locally in the App's private storage. We do not upload the original photo file or its locally stored copy to our servers. A visual representation of screens where the photo appears may be processed by Clarity as described below.

Preferences

Language, unit, appearance, coordinate-visibility, and background preferences are stored locally on your device so the App can remember your choices.

2. Offline Use

GPScope's core GPS and device-sensor features can operate without an internet connection. Precise coordinates, sensor readings, preferences, and selected background files are processed locally for these features. GPS availability and accuracy still depend on your device, surroundings, and satellite reception.

When an internet connection is available, limited analytics information may be transmitted to Microsoft Clarity as described in this Privacy Policy. The Clarity iOS SDK does not upload sessions that occur while the device is offline.

3. Analytics and Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity for Mobile Apps to understand how people interact with GPScope, diagnose usability issues, and improve performance and design. Clarity begins operating when the App starts and may process:

  • product interactions, including screens visited, taps, touches, and similar usage events;
  • low-level visual and interaction signals used to reconstruct app sessions and create heatmaps;
  • performance information used to assess SDK and App stability; and
  • coarse location inferred from the network IP address.

According to Microsoft's App Store privacy guidance, this information is used for analytics, is not linked to your identity, and is not used for cross-app tracking. GPScope does not provide Clarity with a custom user ID. The App masks the precise latitude/longitude card and the custom background as displayed on the main dashboard. Other screens where a selected photo appears, and other information visible in the interface—such as altitude, speed, pressure, settings, and general interaction patterns—may be represented in Clarity's session reconstruction. The original selected photo file is not intentionally transmitted to Clarity.

Clarity sends analytics information to Microsoft when network conditions permit. For more information about Microsoft's handling of information, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Clarity for Mobile Apps documentation.

4. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • provide GPS, compass, speed, altitude, accuracy, pressure, and personalization features;
  • remember your settings and selected background;
  • understand feature usage and improve the App's design and usability;
  • monitor performance, reliability, and security; and
  • respond to support, privacy, or legal requests.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use App information for targeted advertising.

5. How Information Is Shared

We share limited analytics information with Microsoft solely because Microsoft provides the Clarity analytics service. Microsoft processes that information under its applicable terms and privacy commitments. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the security of the App, or as part of a business reorganization where legally permitted and subject to appropriate safeguards.

We require service providers that process information on our behalf, including Microsoft, to provide the same or an equivalent level of protection described in this Privacy Policy and required by applicable Apple policies and data-protection law.

We do not upload precise GPS coordinates or selected background photos to advertisers or data brokers. Local preference files are not uploaded to our servers, although interactions with settings may appear in Clarity analytics.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • On-device information: preferences and a selected background remain until you change or remove them, reset the App's data, or uninstall the App.
  • Live sensor information: GPScope does not create a route or historical location log. Live readings are refreshed as the App runs.
  • Clarity information: Microsoft currently states that session playback data is generally retained for 30 days, while heatmaps, labeled or favorited sessions, and interaction data may be retained for up to 9 months. Microsoft may update these periods under its own retention policy.

You can remove a custom background from GPScope Settings. Deleting the App removes its locally stored information from your device. To request deletion of information we control, contact us using the details below. Because Clarity analytics is not linked to an account or identity, we may need additional technical information and may not always be able to associate an analytics record with a particular person.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Location: grant or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > GPScope.
  • Motion & Fitness: manage applicable sensor access in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness.
  • Coordinates: hide latitude and longitude on the GPScope dashboard from the App's Privacy setting.
  • Photos: GPScope uses Apple's system picker and receives only the item you select. You can remove the saved background in GPScope Settings.
  • Analytics: uninstalling the App stops future Clarity collection. You may also contact us with an analytics objection or deletion request.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection regarding personal information, or to lodge a complaint with a privacy regulator. Contact us to exercise an applicable right. We may need to verify your request and may retain limited information where required by law.

8. International Data Transfers

Microsoft may process Clarity information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, the parties responsible for processing rely on lawful transfer mechanisms and safeguards under applicable data-protection law. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement for more information.

9. Children's Privacy

GPScope is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required by local law. We do not knowingly request a child's name, contact details, or account information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect the information handled through GPScope. However, no device, storage system, or network transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to GPScope, third-party services, or legal requirements. We will post the updated policy at this location and revise the effective date above. If a change materially affects your rights, we will provide additional notice where required.
